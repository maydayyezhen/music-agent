# Composition routing

Define form, harmony, motif and energy before assigning instrumental phrases. Preserve the
musical intent fields required by `docs/instrument_research/architecture_proposal.md`. Do not
write final MIDI notes until the instrument role and phrase type are known.

For an 8–16 bar melody, plan delayed target, register/energy/density arc, cadence locations
and related motif transformations first. Follow `docs/long_form_phrase_schema.md`; adjacent
independent four-bar statements do not count as a long-form section.
