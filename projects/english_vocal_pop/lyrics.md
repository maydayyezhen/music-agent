# Different Windows

Original English lyrics. No named artist or existing song is being imitated.

## Verse 1

Coffee ring on a moving box  
Seven-thirty on the station clock  
I kept your note in my jacket seam  
“A little far isn't out of reach”

## Pre-Chorus 1

Every red light tells me wait  
Every green light says to go

Different windows, same sky  
You turn your lamp on, so do I  
If the miles keep pulling through  
They can't move the moon from you

Someday the boxes turn to shelves  
We stop explaining where we are

You turn your lamp on, so do I  
When the morning opens blue  
I will share the room with you

Leave it burning through the night
