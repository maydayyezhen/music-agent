# Composition Plan — 《送到这盏灯就好》

## Global design

- 114 BPM, 4/4, G major / E minor, 100 bars, approximately 3:32.5 rendered.
- Form: Intro 8 / Verse 1 12 / Pre 1 6 / Chorus 1 12 / Interlude 4 / Verse 2 12 / Pre 2 6 / Chorus 2 12 / Bridge 8 / Final Chorus 14 / Outro 6.
- Melody range: B3-E5. Verses remain mostly B3-G4; pre-choruses climb D4-B4; choruses open G4-D5; only the final chorus reaches E5. Bridge returns to B3-F#4 before rebuilding.
- Hook rhythm: “你送我到 / 这盏灯就好” uses a repeated two-beat pickup-to-landing shape, then changes its final landing in Chorus 2 and Final Chorus.

## Harmony

- Intro / Verse center: Em7-Cadd9-G6-Dsus4/F#, preserving open G/D across shapes.
- Pre-chorus: Am7-Cadd9-G/B-D, then Am7-Cadd9-Dsus4; predominant motion and suspended dominant delay the release.
- Chorus: Cadd9-G6-D-Em7 / Cadd9-G/B-Am7-D / Cadd9-G6-D-Em7. The brighter C→G arrival supports the hook without abandoning the E-minor color.
- Bridge: Em7-D-Cadd9-G/B / Am7-Em7-Cadd9-Dsus4. Descending bass first, then predominant-to-dominant rebuild.
- Final chorus extends the established material by two bars and changes the final connection to Cadd9-D-G6 rather than stopping on Em7.

## Acoustic guitar by section

- Intro: directional arpeggio and bass-first sweeps; open D/G tones carry through changes; bar 8 anticipates the Verse without stopping.
- Verse 1: bass note plus partial down/up cells, muted middle-string ghost strokes, and one fuller sweep in selected lyric breaths. Right-hand motion continues when the vocal sustains.
- Pre 1: six-bar gradient from partially muted four-string sweeps to open five/six-string down/up motion; anticipations begin only in bars 5-6.
- Chorus 1: unequal eight-note sweep cell with alternating partial/full attacks. Chord tones are staggered low-to-high or high-to-low; no block attacks.
- Verse 2: replaces the Verse 1 cell with arpeggio-plus-ghost-upstroke motion, increased high-string content, and different phrase-ending connections.
- Pre 2: begins more open and introduces anticipations one bar earlier than Pre 1.
- Bridge: first half returns to sparse bass/high-string arpeggio; second half progressively reintroduces sweep groups.
- Final Chorus: new five-group pattern in later bars, stronger upstrokes, changed final harmonic connection, then gradual release into Outro.
- Outro: reconstructs the Intro arpeggio and finishes with a natural staggered sustain instead of truncation.

## Electric guitars

Electric Rhythm Guitar is mostly absent or sustained in Verse 1, uses muted low-mid dyads in the pre-choruses, and supplies broad staggered power/partial attacks in choruses. Electric Texture Guitar stays above the acoustic register and uses no more than two sub-one-bar fills per eight bars. It never copies the vocal proxy or acoustic rhythm. In the bridge it nearly disappears; in the final chorus it adds high sustained partials only after the acoustic pulse is already established.

## Rhythm section and final development

Bass uses longer independent notes when acoustic guitar is dense, then adds fifth/octave approaches around long vocal notes and pre-chorus boundaries. Drums move from closed-hat verse backbeat to gradually opening pre-chorus hats, chorus crashes/ride, half-time bridge, and the widest final-chorus cymbal language. Final Chorus adds two bars, altered lyric/melody endings, an extra acoustic attack group, higher electric voicings, more active bass approaches, and the only pad-supported peak.
