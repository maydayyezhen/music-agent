# Energy Map

| Section | Bars | Energy | Entrances / exits | Density and register | Harmony / tension | Drum language | Transition |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 1–4 | 4→6 | Piano states motif; guitar answers; pad and bass enter after the opening; drums begin restrained | Mid/high piano, sparse guitar, bass eighth-note pickups | Dadd9 cycle, open and optimistic | Closed hats, kick/snare; short fill in bar 4 | Snare/tom pickup into Verse |
| Verse | 5–12 | 5→6 | Piano thins; muted guitar pulse and active bass carry walking motion; strings mostly absent | Lead stays around A3–B4 with rests; groove foreground | D–A/C#–Bm–G, stable but with C# bass motion | Tight 8th hats, backbeat, controlled syncopated kicks | Last bar opens hats and adds tom fill |
| Pre-Chorus | 13–20 | 6→8 | Strings enter and climb; piano arpeggiation rises; guitar changes from mute to longer power shapes | Register and subdivision rise across two 4-bar waves | Em–F#m–G–A; dominant withheld at both ends | Denser hats, extra kicks, snare lift; final-bar roll | Held A(sus4→A), brief breath, crash into Chorus |
| Chorus | 21–36 | 10 | Full ensemble; hook in piano/top line, guitar power-chord drive and responses, strings reinforce selected peaks | Highest melody F#5; broad stereo/harmonic field; bass in active eighths | Tonic payoff via D–A/C#–Bm–G and D/F#–G–Em–A | Crash arrivals, open hats, stronger kick, fills at bars 28 and 36 | Final fill spills into Outro while hook remains audible |
| Outro | 37–48 | 8→3 | First 8 bars retain chorus momentum; final 4 strip drums/guitar, leaving piano, strings, pad and bass resolution | Hook fragment descends; density falls decisively | Chorus loop once, then Bm–G–Em–A–Dadd9 cadence | Full groove first half; decrescendo fill; drums exit for last 4 | Long Dadd9 release with a short piano afterglow |

## Perceptual checks

- Pre-Chorus rise comes from climbing string line, faster piano motion, stronger hats and dominant withholding—not only velocity.
- Chorus payoff adds the hook's high register, full bass subdivisions, crash/open-hat language and broader voicings.
- The final four bars remove drums and guitar so the ending reads as emotional resolution rather than another loop.
