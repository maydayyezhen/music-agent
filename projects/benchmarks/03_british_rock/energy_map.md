# Energy Map

| Section | Bars | Energy | Entrances / exits | Density & register | Tension / transition |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Cold Open | 4 | 7/10 | L guitar attacks immediately; R guitar, bass, drums answer; no organ | Riff exposed in mid register; lean but loud | Two-bar riff repeated; tom fill throws into verse |
| Verse | 12 | 8/10 | Full quartet; no organ | L riff active, R chord pulse, mobile bass, eighth hats | Modal E–G–A–G loop; every fourth bar points back with D# bass approach |
| Chorus | 12 | 9/10 | Full quartet | Wider R-guitar triadic power voicings, denser bass, open hats/crashes | D–A–E–G loop releases into E; fills articulate four-bar spans |
| Breakdown | 8 | 5/10 | Organ enters; drums strip to half-time; R guitar becomes short answers | More air, lower event count, sustained organ above guitars | C–G–D–A/B shape with rising final-bar attacks; tom pickup rebuilds momentum |
| Final Chorus | 8 | 10/10 | Full band plus quiet organ glue | Highest velocity and widest stereo guitar image; chorus hook returns | Same harmonic identity, intensified rather than replaced |
| Outro | 2 | 6/10 → stop | Organ exits; quartet makes one riff and final E hit | Density collapses after the last downbeat | Crash/kick and sustained E provide a blunt ending |

Humanization is written through phrase-shaped velocities and unequal articulations. Kick/main snare/downbeats remain grid anchors; hats alternate velocity and guitar attacks vary by role. No broad random timing pass is used because the loose feel comes primarily from syncopation, note lengths, and unequal accents.
