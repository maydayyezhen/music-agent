# Instrument Notes

## Electric Guitar L

GM Overdriven Guitar, hard left. Main foreground voice. Uses playable two-note root/fifth shapes and occasional octave reinforcement between E3 and A4. The recurring rhythm is deliberately more important than harmonic complexity. Notes are short enough to read as struck/muted attacks, not organ blocks.

## Electric Guitar R

The same real GM Overdriven Guitar preset, hard right, but composed independently. It begins with longer replies, becomes chordal in verse/chorus, and changes to sparse single-note responses in the breakdown. V1 intentionally tests a busy verse pulse; the first render will determine whether it masks the left riff.

## Electric Bass

GM Electric Bass (finger), centered. Range E2–G3. It locks selected attacks with kick, then separates through fifths, upper chord tones, offbeat pickups, and chromatic D#/G# approaches. The line is active but avoids constant sixteenth-note filling.

## Drums

GM Standard Drum Kit. Kick/snare establish a direct rock backbeat, while eight-note hats carry forward motion. Chorus adds open hats, more kick activity, and arrival crashes. Breakdown switches to half-time with an economical tom rebuild. No double-kick metal pattern.

## Drawbar Organ

Real GM Drawbar Organ (program 16), used quietly only in breakdown and final chorus. It supplies thin upper-mid glue and held counter-voicings; it is not the harmonic engine and never replaces the guitar riff.
