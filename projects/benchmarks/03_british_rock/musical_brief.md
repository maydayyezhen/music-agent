# Musical Brief — Borrowed Trouble

- **Genre:** British / alternative rock; explicitly not metal.
- **Emotional target:** fast, youthful, slightly unhinged, loose, and mildly rebellious—like a rehearsal-room take that is one coffee past sensible.
- **Key / mode:** E minor with modal rock movement through G, A, C, and D power-chord centers. D# appears only as an approach back to E.
- **Tempo / meter:** 156 BPM, 4/4.
- **Length:** 46 bars, approximately 70.8 seconds of score plus a 2-second render tail.
- **Structure:** Cold Open 4 / Verse 12 / Chorus 12 / Breakdown 8 / Final Chorus 8 / Outro 2.
- **Core instrumentation:** Electric Guitar L, Electric Guitar R, Electric Bass, Standard Drum Kit. Drawbar Organ is a quiet color in the breakdown/final chorus only.
- **Available timbres used:** GeneralUser GS SoundFont only: GM Overdriven Guitar (program 29), Electric Bass Finger (33), Standard Kit (channel 10), Drawbar Organ (16).
- **Production character:** hard-panned overdriven guitars, centered bass/drums, restrained organ, clear transient-driven mix.
- **Core motif:** short-short / gap / short / long / short, voiced as root-fifth power-chord attacks plus octave answers. It returns in every high-energy section and is reharmonized rather than replaced.
- **Exclusions:** no metal double-kick language, no palm-muted chug wall, no orchestral strings/pads, no piano-pop chord bed, no copied piano-to-guitar writing.

## Harmonic plan

- Cold open and verse: E5–G5–A5–G5, centered on an E-minor/modal riff.
- Chorus: D5–A5–E5–G5, opening the register and landing most strongly on E.
- Breakdown: C5–G5–D5–A5/B5 tension, with sparse drums and organ upper voices.
- Outro: E riff, then an emphatic E5/E octave stop.

The guitars have separate jobs: left owns the syncopated identity riff; right supplies wider chord answers and later a sparse counter-line. Bass uses fifths, passing tones, chromatic approaches, anticipations, and occasional rests rather than tracing roots. Drums keep a clear two/four backbeat but change cymbal and kick density by section.
