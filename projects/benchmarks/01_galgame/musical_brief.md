# Musical Brief — Platform Afterglow

- **Style:** late-1990s / early-2000s Japanese Galgame everyday or ending BGM
- **Emotional target:** youthful, nostalgic, gentle, and slightly distant; the feeling of watching a familiar train leave after school at dusk
- **Key / mode:** D major, with B minor and suspended dominant colors
- **Tempo / meter:** 92 BPM, 4/4
- **Length:** 28 bars, about 73 seconds before the render tail
- **Form:** Intro (4) → A (8) → B (8) → Return (6) → Outro (2)
- **Core motif:** F#–A–B–A, first heard as a spacious rising-and-looking-back phrase. Its dotted/short-short rhythmic identity is repeated, raised, compressed, and finally softened.
- **Harmony:** Dmaj9–A/C#–Bm7–Gadd9 establishes warmth; A section develops through F#m7, D/F#, Em7, and Asus; B begins from Bm7 and delays full tonic return until the Return section.
- **Instrumentation:** acoustic piano lead/sparse shells, finger bass, GM clean electric guitar, warm pad, light standard drums, and string ensemble entering only in the second half.
- **Production character:** local GM/SF2 rendering, soft attacks, moderate stereo width, restrained velocities, no glossy modern loudness.
- **Exclusions:** no EDM build/drop, no four-on-the-floor kick, no side-chained pumping, no modern supersaw, no wall-to-wall piano chords, no continuously full ensemble.

## Musical narrative

The intro is a solitary piano memory with faint pad air. A section adds walking bass, clean-guitar arpeggio fragments, and a light side-stick groove. B section moves the melodic register upward, shortens the spaces between answers, introduces singing strings, and briefly feels as though the train might be chased. Return releases that motion back to the original motif; the ending leaves a D-add9 resonance rather than a dramatic cadence.
