# Instrument Notes

- **Strings — GM 48, String Ensemble 1:** main sixteenth-note ostinato uses chord tones plus controlled neighbor notes. Upper phrases carry motif variants; sustained notes are reserved for transitions and always paired with moving inner material.
- **Brass — GM 61, Brass Section:** short marcato attacks on arrivals, dominant warnings, and an augmented motif in Heroic Clash. It rests through Shadow Pass and never doubles the full melody continuously.
- **Piano — GM 0, Acoustic Grand Piano:** broken fifth/tenth shapes and off-beat answers. Low notes stay open; right-hand fragments clarify harmony without copying the string ostinato.
- **Bass — GM 33, Electric Bass (finger):** roots anchor downbeats, fifths and passing/approach notes connect each harmony. Important attacks cooperate with the kick but include eighth-note pickups.
- **Drums — Standard Drum Kit on channel 10:** tight kick/snare anchors; velocity-shaped hats, restrained ghost notes, tom fills at boundaries, ride and crashes reserved for Heroic Clash.
- The local song-level mapping intentionally uses only programs available through the existing `GeneralUser-GS.sf2`; no external or imagined patches are referenced.
