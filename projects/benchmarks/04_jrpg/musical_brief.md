# Musical Brief — Runeblade Pursuit

- Style: 1990s–2000s JRPG adventure/battle field music; urgent orchestral MIDI character, not an anime opening.
- Emotional target: forward adventure, tightening danger, decisive heroic release.
- Key / mode: D minor, with harmonic-minor C# at dominant cadences and brief F-major brightness in the final road.
- Tempo / meter: 152 BPM, 4/4.
- Planned length: 48 bars, about 75.8 seconds before renderer tail; five sections.

- Harmony: functional minor motion centered on Dm, Bb, Gm and A/A7. The dominant is intensified rather than replaced by unrelated harmony.
- Instrumentation: String Ensemble 1, Brass Section, Acoustic Grand Piano, Electric Bass (finger), Standard Drum Kit. Every timbre is present in the local GeneralUser GS mapping.
- Production character: compact console-era orchestral palette, clear attacks, rhythmic ostinato, limited ambience, strong section punctuation.
