# Energy Map

| Section | Energy | Entrances / exits | Density and register | Harmony and transition |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Frontier Call | 4/10 | Piano and low strings establish motion; bass enters after the opening gesture; drums are restrained; brass marks bar 4 only. | Eighth-note piano pulse, short string replies, middle register, velocities 58–92. | Dm–Bb–Gm–A; held dominant and tom pickup launch Pursuit. |
| Pursuit | 7/10 | Full string ostinato, bass and kit; piano answers every second bar; brass only on phrase boundaries. | Continuous but accented sixteenth-note strings, active bass eighths, melody D5–A5, velocities 66–108. | Dm/Bb/Gm/A cycles with stronger A7 arrivals; fill at bars 8 and 12. |
| Shadow Pass | 5/10 | Brass exits; hats thin to eighths; piano moves to low broken shells; upper strings become a rising counter-line. | Lower melody register and more rests; ostinato changes contour instead of disappearing. | Gm–Dm/F–Bb–A, then pedal A; snare/tom crescendo points to clash. |
| Heroic Clash | 10/10 | Brass returns as stabs and motif augmentation; drums add ride/crash; strings split foreground motif and lower ostinato roles. | Widest register, highest motif reaches D6 only near the climax; velocities 76–118. | Dm–C–Bb–A and Gm–Bb–A–A7; dominant acceleration drives each four-bar unit. |
| Victory Road | 7→5/10 | Ride drops out after bar 4; brass turns from stabs to cadence tones; piano and strings retain motion, then shorten the final release. | Upper-register answer briefly brightens to F major; texture sheds layers over last two bars. | Bb–F/A–Gm–A then Dm–Bb–A–Dm; final tonic is decisive, not a long ambient fade. |
