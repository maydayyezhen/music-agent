## Lead development

- **Launch:** incomplete three-note signals, creating recognition before completion.
- **Orbital Garden:** full eight-bar theme in D4–F5.
- **Prism Build:** two-bar fragments sequentially raised and rhythmically shortened.
- **Zero Gravity:** augmentation, wider gaps and partial inversion over a D pedal.
- **Wormhole:** compressed offbeat calls, reordered `D–C–A–E` and chromatic neighbor tension.
- **Apogee Bloom:** complete theme one octave higher with an extended A5 landing.
- **Re-entry:** descending reduction `F–E–C–D`, then `A–D` echoes.

## Arrangement boundaries

The lead remains dry enough to read clearly against slow pads. Bell Piano and Synth Chime answer only in lead rests. Acid Bass is busiest in Wormhole/Apogee and almost disappears in Zero Gravity. Pads use different registers and motion so they do not become duplicated chord blocks.
