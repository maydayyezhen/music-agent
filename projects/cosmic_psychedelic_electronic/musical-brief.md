# Musical Brief — *Parallax Bloom*

- **Genre:** instrumental cosmic psychedelic electronic; progressive, melodic and spacious rather than a static dance loop.
- **Emotional arc:** launch curiosity → hypnotic orbital confidence → refracted tension → weightless suspension → turbulent wormhole → radiant apogee → dissolving re-entry.
- **Key / mode:** D Dorian, with modal pivots through C major and A minor; occasional chromatic approach tones represent spatial distortion.
- **Tempo / meter:** 112 BPM, 4/4.
- **Length:** 68 bars, about 145.7 seconds of score plus 2 seconds of render tail; comfortably over two minutes.
- **Form:** Launch 8 → Orbital Garden 12 → Prism Build 8 → Zero Gravity 8 → Wormhole 12 → Apogee Bloom 12 → Re-entry 8.
- **Lead instrument:** **Doctor Solo, SoundFont bank 8 / program 81**. It exclusively owns the foreground theme.
- **Core theme:** pitch cell `D–A–C–E` followed by `F–E–C–D`; rhythmic fingerprint short–short–long–held / short–short–short–landing. It appears as a fragment in Launch, full statement in Orbital Garden, augmentation/inversion in Zero Gravity, compressed syncopation in Wormhole, and octave-raised expansion at Apogee.
- **Harmony:** modal cycles are developed rather than replaced randomly: `Dm9–G13–Cmaj9–Am7`, then `Bbmaj7–Cadd9–Dm9–G`, suspended pedal-D fields in Zero Gravity, and dominant-like A/E tension into Apogee.
- **Palette:** catalog-verified GeneralUser GS presets only: Doctor Solo 8/81, Acid Bass 8/38, Bell Piano 12/0, Solar Wind 11/89, Synth Chime 11/98, Night Vision 13/88, Standard 1 drums 128/0.
- **Production character:** wide slow pads, mallet-like arpeggio constellations, acid-bass movement, dry centered lead, electronic four-on-floor sections alternating with half-time/beatless space, transition fills and deliberate instrumental exits.
- **Exclusions:** no vocals, lyrics, `vocals.json`, or fake vocal layer; no SoundFont preset is represented as a singing backend. Choir/Voice/Vox-labeled presets are valid synthesizer colors under the corrected boundary, but this arrangement independently chooses other timbres. No copied project material, simple unchanged loop stack, or continuously full arrangement.

## Complexity promise

Each section has its own harmonic rhythm, drum language, bass density and thematic transformation. The 12-bar sections contain an 8-bar proposition plus a 4-bar developmental tail, while Zero Gravity removes drums and reduces bass to long pedal gestures before Wormhole rebuilds from syncopated fragments.
