## Perceptual targets

- Orbital Garden must be measurably stronger than Launch.
- Prism Build must rise without making the lead continuous.
- Zero Gravity should be the clearest contrast: nearly beatless and lower RMS.
- Apogee Bloom should be the loudest sustained section and reserve the lead's highest register.
- Re-entry must remove rhythm layers rather than merely lower their velocity.
