# Old Demo vs Knowledge Demo

Both candidates follow the same brief: 2000s Japanese galgame/anime OP instrumental, A major, 120 BPM, about one minute, with Piano, Bass, Guitar, Strings, Pad, and Drums. `knowledge_demo` was composed from a new motif/harmony/energy plan; no old MIDI was copied.

## Direct listening files

- Old: `projects/old_demo/output/mix.wav`
- Knowledge final (v3): `projects/knowledge_demo/output/mix.wav`
- Knowledge first render (before critique revisions): `projects/knowledge_demo/output/mix_v1.wav`

All are 66-second, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo PCM WAV files.

## Composition decisions

| Area | Old demo | Knowledge demo final |
|---|---|---|
| Planning | Composition written directly into sections | Musical brief, motif/harmony test, energy map, instrument roles, render critique, v2/v3 revisions |
| Melody | Several sequential high notes and arpeggios | Declared pickup + short–short–long motif; lower incomplete Verse form; full Guitar Chorus hook; A′ answer and one C#6 climax |
| Harmony | Mostly root-position repeated blocks | Aadd9/E-G#/F#m7/Dadd9 world developed with inversions, common tones, maj7/add9/sus colors, and dominant preparation |
| Bass | Has movement, but fewer velocity states and mostly repeating routes | Roots plus fifths/chord tones/passing and approach notes; 23 velocity values; specific lines aimed at the next chord |
| Guitar | Present in Intro and Verse; Chorus primarily continuous lead | Completely withheld in Intro, muted off-beat power shapes only in late Verse, sustained Pre, then owns the hook with sparse chord punctuation |
| Piano | Prominent chord/arpeggio role across the form | Motif and question/answer early; deliberately switches to broken-chord drive under the Guitar hook |
| Strings | 144 total notes; 84 in Chorus, often dense sustained harmony | 58 total notes; 32 in Chorus; delayed answers, a Verse inner voice, reduced Pre, then spaced A′ counter-line/climax |
| Pad | Continuous section support | Remains subordinate and intentionally rests in final Pre bar before returning for Chorus width |
| Drums | Main snare generally on beat 3; 237 notes, 27 velocity values | 2/4 backbeat, shaped hats, section-dependent kicks/open hats/fills; 369 notes, 59 velocity values |
| Arrangement | Every instrument has Intro events | Guitar absent from Intro; Strings delayed; late Verse guitar entrance; Pad pre-chorus dropout; withheld high register and full drums until Chorus |

## Revision caused by critique

After rendering v1, the critique found a half-time snare inconsistent with the target, a weak Pre-to-Chorus jump, Strings that peaked too early and talked continuously, underweighted Piano drive, and a late duplicate fill collision introduced during v2.

The revisions moved the principal snare to beats 2/4, thinned the first half of Pre, made Chorus Piano accents stronger, reduced Strings from 95 to 58 events and created rests, raised only the A′ string climax, lowered the final crash, and deduplicated the Pre fill. The motif, harmony, form, and brief were retained so revision developed the song instead of replacing it.
