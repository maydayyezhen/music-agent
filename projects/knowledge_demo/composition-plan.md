# Knowledge Demo — Composition Plan

This plan is written before MIDI expansion and is the source of truth for critique.

## Motif + harmony test

The core motif uses only A-major chord/extension tones over Aadd9. Over E/G#, the held E becomes the root and G# becomes a chord tone; over F#m7, A/F#/E remain chord tones; over Dmaj9, E is the 9th and resolves naturally. It therefore survives reharmonization without being a scale exercise.

The phrase's highest note is initially A5. C#6 is withheld for the A′ chorus climax. The Verse uses a lower octave and incomplete answers so the Chorus can feel like a remembered idea finally stated in full.

## Voice-leading anchors

- Aadd9 → E/G#: retain E and B; move C# to B/G# as needed.
- E/G# → F#m7: G# bass rises to F# by register shift while upper E remains common; B can move to A/C#.
- F#m7 → Dmaj7: retain F#/A/C#; introduce D in a supporting voice.
- Bm7 → Esus4 → E: retain B/E/A, then resolve A to G# for the dominant release.

## Transition markers

- Intro → Verse: small high-tom/snare pickup; no crash.
- Verse → Pre: open hat and rising bass approach.
- Pre → Chorus: last-beat tom/snare fill, a short arrangement breath, then crash + hook pickup.
- Chorus A → A′: crash plus altered bass pickup, not a full reset.
- Ending: final A-major landing, drum cymbal tail, no extra 30-second SoundFont release.

## Draft policy

`composition_v1.json` is the first complete realization. It must be rendered and evaluated before edits. Findings go to `critique.md`; all accepted changes become `composition_v2.json`, which is then copied to `composition.json` for final rendering.
