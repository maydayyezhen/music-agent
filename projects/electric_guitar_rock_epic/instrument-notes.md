# Instrument Notes

- `lead_guitar` — GeneralUser GS bank 0/program 30 Distortion Guitar. Main melody only. Long-short verse fingerprint; compressed eighth-note chorus hook. Fully silent in bars 57–72.
- `rhythm_guitar` — bank 0/program 29 Overdrive Guitar. Playable two/three-note power shapes, sparse held/answered attacks in verses, five-hit pulses in choruses, progressively tighter build ostinato. It never duplicates the lead pitch line.
- `bass` — bank 0/program 34 Pick Bass. Roots, fifths, inversions, anticipations, and next-chord pickups. In the no-lead build it becomes a foreground counterline: 2 attacks/bar → 4 → 6.
- `organ` — bank 0/program 18 Rock Organ. Plane in spacious sections, chord answers in choruses, then an independent rising two-note and three-note counter-melody through bars 57–72.
- `strings` — GeneralUser GS bank 8/program 48 Orchestra Pad. Sustained plane, common-tone-oriented emotional support, upper chord-tone line at climaxes; intentionally not another block-piano copy.
- `drums` — bank 128/program 16 Power kit on channel 10. Sparse half-time verse language, faster chorus hats/kicks, structural fills only; bars 57–72 develop low→mid→high tom vocabulary into the last pre-impact fill.

No real guitar plugin or alternate SoundFont is present/connected in the repository. These are catalog-verified GeneralUser GS presets through FluidSynth, not claimed amp plugins.
