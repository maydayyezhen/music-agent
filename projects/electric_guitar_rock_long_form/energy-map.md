# Energy Map

| Section | Target | Density / register / drums / tension |
|---|---:|---|
| Intro | 0.28 | half-note guitar pulse, low-mid bass, light verse kit, narrow strings |
| Verse 1 | 0.42 | quarter-note muted guitar with regular holes; lead breaths; no strings |
| Pre-Chorus | 0.61 | eighth-note mute, rising lead target, fuller bass approaches, transition fill |
| Chorus 1 | 0.84 | open power chords on eighths, denser kicks/hats, lead peak in bar 12 |
| Verse 2 | 0.48 | slow feel returns, changed harmony and slightly higher lead arc |
| Bridge Void | 0.36 | lead zero; sparse power pulse; organ answers and bass connects |
| Bridge Build | 0.76 | lead still zero; rhythm/bass/organ/strings all rise, chorus kit drives |
| Final Chorus | 1.00 | transformed hook, delayed bar-14 peak, widest strings, strongest subdivision |
| Outro | 0.34 | slow pulse, soft kit, lower register, final B-minor release |

The two Bridge halves deliberately change onset density and register. The first half is conversational; the second turns those lines into a shared ascent. Final Chorus is not merely louder Chorus 1: its lead target, peak bar, motif operations, rhythmic-guitar rest pattern and string register all differ.
