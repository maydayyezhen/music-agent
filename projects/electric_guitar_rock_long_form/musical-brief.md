# Musical Brief — When the Horizon Answers

- Genre: emotional instrumental rock with cinematic color.
- Key / tempo: B minor, 108 BPM, 4/4. The tempo stays fixed; the perceived Verse/Chorus speed contrast comes from subdivision and drum language.
- Target length: 96 bars, approximately 3:33 plus render tail.
- Emotional line: distant preparation -> restrained longing -> first eruption -> withdrawal -> communal bridge build -> largest, moving return -> quiet answer.
- Primary narrator: Distortion Guitar lead. Its long sections use the long-form planner, not repeated closed four-bar motifs.
- Supporting players: Overdriven Guitar, fingered electric bass, Power Kit, Rock Organ and string ensemble, rendered locally through GeneralUser GS and FluidSynth.
- Exclusions: no vocals, no `vocals.json`, no cloud generation, no Ample/VST claim, and no reading/reuse of previous finished composer projects.

## Core material

The `horizon_call` rhythm is a spacious long-short cell with cross-bar continuations. The chorus compresses it into `chorus_drive`; the identity remains in the ascent and delayed held target. B-minor harmony deliberately alternates i–VI–III–VII colors with pre-dominant Em/G and F# tension.

## Silence plan

Verses omit fast guitar subdivision and leave phrase breaths. Strings are entirely absent in both verses. The 16-bar Bridge removes Lead Guitar completely. Outro reduces subdivision and active foreground roles.
