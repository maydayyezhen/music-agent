# Instrument Notes

## Lead Guitar — foreground line

Long-form section arcs cover complete 8–16 bar windows. The motif crosses bar lines, only the final relation resolves strongly, and breaths preserve melodic state. Verse phrases use sparse calls; choruses compress the rhythm. The Bridge has no Lead Guitar clip at all.

## Rhythm Guitar — pulse / plane

Verses use palm-muted quarter-note actions with explicit omissions. Choruses open into eighth-note power chords. Bridge Void leaves alternating spaces; Bridge Build moves from sparse eighth-note actions toward a denser second half.

## Bass — continuous line

Every span contains root support plus fifth/octave/approach functions. Important attacks cooperate with kick, but the approach at 3.25 gives the bass its own destination. In the Bridge it is a low-register relay voice, not a duplicated rhythm-guitar root track.

## Drums — structural rhythm

Verse kit uses restrained timekeeping and varied kick turns. Chorus/Bridge Build use eighth hats, additional kicks and boundary fills. Fills replace hand timekeeping to preserve physical feasibility.

## Organ — plane becomes line

Outside Bridge it uses voice-led sustained chords. During Bridge it changes to a generated counterline plus a sparse authored answer; the second half rises in register, increases onsets and ends on a higher pickup.

## Strings — long emotional counter-motion

Strings are withheld from verses. They retain common tones in Intro/Pre/Chorus and become an ascending emotional lane across both Bridge halves. Final Chorus moves them into their widest/highest register.
