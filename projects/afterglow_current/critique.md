# Afterglow Current critique

## V1 structural review

The first draft established a coherent two-guitar hierarchy and retained one recognizable lead cell, but two authored decisions were too eager:

1. Theme A filled the end of its first four-bar statement without a real breath.
2. The Peak reached its highest B5 target in the sixth bar, leaving too much time after the climax.

These are composition-level findings from the event plan. They are not claims about the rendered SoundFont audio.

## V2 revision

- Theme A now ends its opening statement at beat 14.75, leaving a visible breath before the answer begins at beat 16.
- The highest B5 bend target moves from beat 21.5 to beat 25.5 of the Peak, placing it in the seventh bar.
- The post-peak descent is shortened and made more direct so the target behaves as a climax rather than the start of another unrelated lick.
- Acoustic strumming remains foreground-aware and continuous across bar and chord boundaries.
