# Energy Map

| Section | Bars | Energy | Strumming behavior |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Intro | 4 | 3 | Acoustic steady eighths alone; Organ enters late |
| Long Verse | 12 | 5 | Acoustic full hand grid; Electric muted complementary grid |
| Lift | 8 | 7 | Both guitars remain continuous and gradually open |
| First Chorus | 12 | 8 | Open Acoustic sweep plus broad Electric power-strum pattern |
| Instrumental Run | 8 | 7 | Continuous guitars under an active Organ counterline |
| Final Chorus | 16 | 9 | Longest uninterrupted payoff; Organ rises in final four bars |
| Outro | 4 | 4 | Acoustic remains in motion through the last bar; Electric releases |

Energy comes from openness, accents, register and additional layers—not by restarting the guitar hand at each section.
