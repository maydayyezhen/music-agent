# Instrument Notes

- Acoustic Steel Guitar: left side, 64/64 bars, alternating eighth-note hand motion. Muted/partial/full actions change by section but there is no sectional hand reset.
- Electric Rhythm Guitar: right side, 60 consecutive bars after Intro. Low power-shape voicing, shorter gate in Verse/Lift, broader Chorus release, independent pattern from Acoustic.
- Drawbar Organ: main melody. Uses a rising six-note identity, rests at phrase ends and a high-register Final Chorus development.
- Pick Bass: root/fifth/octave motion with stronger four-beat drive in Chorus; supports chord connection without copying every guitar stroke.
- Power Drums: steady eighth subdivision, clear backbeat, section-boundary fills and Chorus crashes.
- Orchestra Pad: enters late as a plane; never replaces guitar motion and stays below the foreground balance.
