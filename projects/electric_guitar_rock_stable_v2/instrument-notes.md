# Instrument Notes

## Lead Guitar

- Primary narrator; every active clip explicitly uses `phrase_generation_mode: legacy_stable`.
- Strictly monophonic, mostly clean picked sustain. A few semantic `slide` labels identify
  phrase arrivals but General MIDI does not invent a keyswitch or Pitch Bend for them.
- Intro/verse state the D–F–A–G lantern call; Pre transposes it upward; Chorus compresses it;
  Final Chorus raises and changes the ending. Bridge has no clip and therefore zero events.

## Rhythm Guitar

- Muted, perforated eighths in verses; open power chords in choruses.
- Bridge is a pedal-riff carrier: same physical guitar generator, but its repeated power shapes
  and denser second-level pulse become one part of the build rather than mere background.
- Strum ordering is physical and generated by the instrument-aware compiler.

## Bass

- Root anchors, fifths, octave motion and approach notes; important attacks lock to kick but
  pickups remain independent.
- During the bridge the line is explicitly promoted to “rising relay line” and connects every
  harmony change while the upper counterlines climb.

## Drums

- Verse kit uses quarter hats and sparse kick turns; chorus uses eighth hats, additional kick
  syncopation and structural crashes.
- Bridge uses chorus-level propulsion with a final one-beat tom/snare handoff.
- Fills replace hand timekeeping, preserving the two-hand/two-foot model.

## Organ

- Voice-led three-note plane outside the bridge, absent in Verse 1 to preserve space.
- Bridge bars 1–8: two attacks per bar and a measured ascending melody.
- Bridge bars 9–16: three attacks per bar, higher register, shorter durations and a final pickup.

## Strings

- Common-tone plane and inner motion outside the bridge.
- Bridge answers the organ on displaced beats; activity doubles from one to two notes per bar in
  the second half and reaches D6 as the final-chorus lead returns.
