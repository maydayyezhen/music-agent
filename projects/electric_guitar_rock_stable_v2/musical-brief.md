# Musical Brief — Lanterns Against the Rain

## Intent

An instrumental emotional rock piece about one warm light remaining visible through a storm.
The lead electric guitar is the narrator. The arc is restraint → first release → renewed doubt →
a sixteen-bar handoff with no lead guitar → the largest, most moving final release.

## Fixed parameters

- Key: D minor
- Tempo: 106 BPM, 4/4
- Length: 96 bars, approximately 3:37 before renderer tail
- Timbres: GM Distortion Guitar lead, Overdriven rhythm guitar, fingered electric bass,
  rock drums, rock organ, string ensemble
- Renderer: local FluidSynth with GeneralUser GS only
- Exclusions: vocals, Ample/VST claims, Long-Form experimental realization, automatic
  vibrato, decorative Pitch Bend, unexplained cross-bar elongation, overlapping lead notes

## Structure and narrative

| Section | Bars | Function |
|---|---:|---|
| Intro | 8 | Rain and distant lantern; theme enters only in bars 5–8 |
| Verse 1 | 12 | Slow-feeling question/answer with two-bar breaths |
| Pre-Chorus | 8 | Ascending transpositions and shorter waiting time |
| Chorus 1 | 16 | First open-chord release and quicker melodic subdivision |
| Verse 2 | 12 | Familiar motif, then increasingly incomplete fragments |
| Bridge Relay | 16 | Lead guitar entirely absent; four other players carry a rising relay |
| Final Chorus | 16 | Highest energy, altered harmony, octave expansion and new closing run |
| Outro | 8 | Theme returns an octave lower and settles on D |

## Theme and harmony

The core rhythm is long–short–short–held: beat 1 for 1.25 beats, then attacks around 2.5,
3.25 and 4. Its first pitch form outlines D–F–A before falling to G. Answers contract toward
D/C. Chorus compresses the same contour to five attacks per bar and aims at C6 before release.

The verse uses Dm–Bb–F–C. Pre-Chorus uses Gm–Bb–C–A to withhold tonic. Chorus begins
Bb–F–C–Dm; its second half uses A as a clear dominant. The final return replaces the middle
repeat with Gm–Bb–F–C and delays the last D-minor landing until bar 16.

## Complexity profile

Global `rich`: rhythm 4, harmony 3, arrangement 5, ornamentation 3, density 3, variation 5.
The complexity is spent on sectional role exchange and meaningful return variants, not on
continuous activity. The lead is absent for sixteen bars, Verse 1 omits organ, and melodic
parts leave two-bar-scale breaths.
