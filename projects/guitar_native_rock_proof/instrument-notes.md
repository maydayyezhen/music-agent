# Instrument notes

- Lead Guitar: one continuous monophonic line. Authored notes carry planned zero-based string/fret coordinates and position labels (`mid_7`, `upper_12`, `high_17`). Hammer/pull is used only for reachable same-string movement; slides mark position changes; bends are channel-safe because no other lead note is active.
- Rhythm Guitar: palm-muted eighth-note right-hand motion in tighter sections; open, longer power-chord sweeps in Theme B and Final Theme. It stays below the lead register.
- Bass: pick articulation, root/fifth/octave/approach vocabulary, selected kick alignment rather than exact duplication.
- Drums: Power kit, clear snare on 2/4, sectional hat/crash language, playable transition fills.
- Organ: three-voice upper plane only in Bridge, Solo, Final Theme and Outro; it does not copy the lead.
