# Britpop Partial Chord Motion

## Identity

A guitar-voicing family that creates motion inside otherwise simple harmony by moving only part of the chord.

Useful ingredients include:

```text
partial chords
diads
inversions
common tones
open-string continuity
small hammer-on / pull-off embellishment
upper-voice movement
inner-voice movement
```

The purpose is not harmonic complexity for its own sake. The purpose is to give a familiar progression a distinct guitar-shaped surface.

## Core principle

Separate:

```text
chord identity
from
voicing identity
```

A simple harmonic progression can still have character when the guitar preserves common tones and moves one or two voices around them.

Think:

```text
stable notes
+ one changing voice
```

or:

```text
compact dyad
-> nearby dyad
-> fuller chord only at arrival
```

rather than replaying the same full-position chord on every attack.

## Motion vocabulary

Useful local operations:

```text
hold a common upper tone while bass / inner voices change
move one chord member by step
replace full chord with 2–3 note fragment
use inversion to reduce register jump
brief hammer-on into a chord tone
brief pull-off from an upper embellishing tone
allow a compatible open string to ring through a change
```

These are options, not a checklist.

## Relationship to melody

Do not let moving guitar voices collide with the foreground melody.

When the melody is active in the same register, simplify the guitar surface or move the guitar identity into an inner voice.

When the melody holds or rests, a small upper-voice guitar motion can become more audible.

## Register and density

Partial voicings are especially useful when full six-string or large block voicings would overcrowd the midrange.

Possible density arc:

```text
verse: 2–3 note fragments
lift: add a common-tone or open-string extension
chorus: combine partial movement with a larger chord body from another guitar
```

Do not assume the chorus must use wider voicings if the song benefits from staying compact.

## Failure modes

Revise when:

- every chord receives decorative hammer-ons regardless of phrase role;
- open strings are retained despite harmonic clashes;
- partial voicings obscure the intended harmony;
- the guitar becomes a busy contrapuntal lead;
- inversion choice creates awkward bass competition;
- the same decorative figure is pasted on every chord;
- this material is treated as the definition of Britpop.

## Study provenance

Promoted from practical Britpop guitar-study evidence. The material retains only the reusable idea of **partial voicing plus local voice motion**. No source chord sequence, riff, or exact fingering pattern is preserved.
