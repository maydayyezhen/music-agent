# Britpop Melodic Half Arpeggio

## Identity

A guitar accompaniment family that sits between block-chord strumming and a fully exposed arpeggio.

The guitar reveals only part of the harmony at a time, often allowing one or two notes to behave melodically while the chord identity remains clear.

Useful tags:

```text
britpop

melodic-accompaniment

```

## Core principle

Do not think:

```text
chord
-> play every chord tone together
```

Instead allow:

```text
stable chord identity
+
small ordered note group
+
one locally memorable upper or inner tone
```

The result should feel like accompaniment with melodic personality, not a second lead melody.

## Structural behavior

A useful phrase can alternate among:

```text
small chord attack
-> 1–3 separated tones
-> partial return
-> breath / sustain
```

or:

```text
bass / middle tone
-> upper chord fragment
-> repeated common tone
-> next harmony
```

Exact rhythm is song-specific. Do not encode one fixed picking pattern.

## Harmonic continuity

Prefer common tones and small register movement when available.

If the harmony changes but one upper note can ring naturally across the change, that continuity may become part of the guitar identity.

Do not preserve open or common tones when they create an unwanted clash with the authored melody or harmony.

## Relationship to melody

This material is useful when the main melody needs space but plain block chords feel generic.

The guitar can occupy gaps in the lead line without constantly answering every phrase.

Good behavior:

```text
lead active
-> guitar fragment simpler / lower pressure

lead rests
-> guitar fragment may become slightly more audible or melodic
```

Avoid filling every vocal or lead rest with a new lick.

## Section role

Possible uses:

```text
verse identity
intro hook support
pre-chorus connective texture
thin chorus layer under a larger rhythm guitar
outro recall
```

It does not need to run through the entire song.

## Realization notes

On MIDI or generic guitar patches, preserve the distinction between:

```text
attack sequence
and
ringing duration
```

Do not turn every separated onset into a clipped staccato note unless the arrangement asks for it.

Small onset offsets may be used for playable chord fragments, but do not add random timing merely to imitate human playing.

## Failure modes

Revise when:

- it becomes a classical-style continuous arpeggio unrelated to the song groove;
- every chord is decomposed in exactly the same order;
- the guitar competes with the main melody;
- every gap is filled;
- all notes are short and detached, destroying the ringing identity;
- the material is treated as a mandatory Britpop pattern.
