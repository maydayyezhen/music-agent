# Britpop Two Guitar Role Contrast

## Identity

A two-guitar arrangement family in which the guitars share the section without simply cloning the same rhythm, register and voicing.

Typical role split:

```text
Guitar A
-> chord body / rhythmic foundation / continuity

Guitar B
-> motif / partial chord / arpeggiated fragment / answering color
```

The exact tones, rhythm, effects and register are song-specific.

## Core principle

Two guitars should increase **role richness**, not just MIDI note count.

Avoid:

```text
same chord
same octave
same rhythm
same attack
same density
```

unless deliberate doubling is needed at a specific arrival.

## Role-pair families

Useful combinations include:

```text
steady chord body + melodic half-arpeggio

ringing clean rhythm + sparse dirty motif

compact partial chords + sustained overdrive support

muted rhythmic pulse + open ringing answers

lower chord mass + upper diad movement
```

Choose one relationship that serves the section. Do not stack every option.

## Foreground hierarchy

When a vocal / flute / lead melody is active:

```text
lead melody = foreground
Guitar A = structural support
Guitar B = secondary identity
```

Guitar B may answer in rests, reinforce a hook ending, or maintain a small recurring motif.

Do not create continuous counterpoint merely because a second guitar exists.

## Section development

A two-guitar arrangement can create section contrast without changing the underlying song idea.

Example role arc:

```text
verse
Guitar A only

pre / lift
Guitar B enters with small recurring figure

chorus
A gains body
B keeps distinct register / motif rather than copying A

break
A drops
B briefly carries recognizable guitar identity
```

This is a structural example, not a required form.

## Density control

If both guitars occupy the midrange, reduce one of:

```text
note count
sustain
attack density
register overlap
velocity / foreground pressure
```

Do not solve every masking problem with hard panning alone.

## Failure modes

Revise when:

- Guitar B is merely a louder duplicate of Guitar A;
- both guitars compete with the lead melody;
- all section lifts are achieved by adding another full chord layer;
- every empty space receives a guitar response;
- the second guitar changes identity every bar with no recurring behavior;
- register separation is used mechanically rather than musically.

## Study provenance

Promoted from the Britpop source study and practical guitar sources. The reusable abstraction is **role-separated guitar layering**, not any source song's exact arrangement.
